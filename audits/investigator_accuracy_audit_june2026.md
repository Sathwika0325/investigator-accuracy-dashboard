## Key Observations

- **Strongest performance — data-model-grounded fixes:** The Investigator was consistently accurate on inventory/variant corrections (#81902, #82099, #82240, #82263), proof/proof_id data fixes (#81714), pricing/tax back-outs (#81629, #81971), and clean root-cause code bugs it could localize (#81600 regex, #81912 stripAllHtml, #81502). When the answer lived in a table row or a specific method, the AI usually nailed it and the dev applied the exact fix.

- **Reorder/versioning family:** June had a cluster of instant-reorder tickets (#81600, #81736, #82108, #82130). The AI handled these well once the pattern was established, correctly linking duplicates (#82108 → #82130) and matching the dev's same-day fix on version-suffix stripping.

- **Most common failure mode — optimistic locking and NPEs framed as timeouts/config:** Several misses came from attributing Hibernate `ObjectOptimisticLockingFailureException`/StaleState or NullPointerExceptions to timeouts, latency, or config. Examples: #81690 and #82104 (actual: optimistic-locking on a PATCH), #81989 (actual: NPE on null default_selected), #82114 (blamed a Lambda ConnectTimeout when no location event was ever created). Correlated infra signals repeatedly pulled the AI away from the real code/front-end cause.

- **Over-engineering simple answers:** In #82171 the AI proposed a manual Cognito provisioning flow when the correct answer was the self-service "request access" path, and in #82349 it produced a valid raw REST endpoint when the team chose a Gemini + Data Connector approach instead. The AI's technical depth sometimes exceeded the mundane resolution.

- **"By design" underestimates:** In #81695 and #81831 the AI leaned toward "working as designed" when the team treated the behavior as a fixable config/over-broad-match problem, landing those at Partially Accurate.

- **Deleted/unconfirmed tickets:** Two well-reasoned analyses (#82077 backlog/DLQ, #82165 job-router DLQ) landed on deleted tickets with no human confirmation. They're rated on the strength and specificity of the analysis rather than a validated outcome.
